FAQ

How do I join PCES?

Reach out to a club officer — membership is added by admins, not self-signup.

Do I need to know how to code already?

No — PCES welcomes members at all skill levels.

What is Hackatime?

A free tool that automatically tracks your coding time.

Is my data private?

Yes — member data is only visible to club leadership within their own section, plus the President and Faculty Advisor.
