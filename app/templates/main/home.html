How to join

PCES membership is by invitation from club leadership. If you're a student interested in joining, reach out to a PCES officer or your school's coding club contact — an admin will add you and you'll be able to log in with your Hack Club identity.

FAQ

How do I join PCES? Reach out to a club officer — membership is added by admins, not self-signup.

Do I need to know how to code already? No — PCES welcomes members at all skill levels.

What is Hackatime? A free tool that automatically tracks your coding time, which we use to recognize members' work.

Is my data private? Yes — member data is only visible to club leadership within their own section, plus the President and Faculty Advisor.
